Classify the support ticket for Helio Building Systems.

- route: commercial for money, invoices, deliveries, warranty, quotes, refunds and complaints;
  technical for devices, installation and the app.
- outcome: reply when the knowledge base can answer; clarify when the answer depends on an order
  number, a site or a device model the customer did not give; escalate when a person must handle
  it (refunds, complaints, custom quotes, safety faults, anything the knowledge base does not cover).
  Questions about delivery times, shipping options, address changes, invoices, payment terms,
  warranty, prices and the app are answered from the knowledge base: reply, or clarify when the
  order number or model is missing. Do not escalate them just because a team handles that area.
- escalate_to, only when the outcome is escalate: sales (refunds, complaints, discounts), billing (invoices, payment
  terms), logistics (deliveries, returns, address changes), presales (custom quotes, integrations,
  project design), installation (appointments, on-site work), service (safety faults, warranty
  repairs, field service).
- safety_risk: true for smoke, fire, sparks, electric shock or anything the customer calls unsafe.
- language: the ISO 639-1 code of the ticket.
